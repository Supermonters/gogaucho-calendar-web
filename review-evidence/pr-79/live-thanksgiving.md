# Live Google Calendar verification

The three screenshots compare weeks in the same exported calendar.

| Week | What to look for |
| --- | --- |

| [November 9–15, Veterans Day week](./02-veterans-day-week.png) | Wednesday, November 11 is empty. Classes on the surrounding days still appear. |
| [November 23–29, Thanksgiving week](./03-thanksgiving-week.png) | Thursday, November 26 is empty and Friday, November 27 has no enrolled classes. The custom Friday event still appears. |
